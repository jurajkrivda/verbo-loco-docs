Learning data stays on your device. Optional synchronization uses your personal iCloud account to keep favorites, practice history and learning settings across devices. No Verbo Loco account, advertising or analytics tracking.

Support

For questions or a problem with Verbo Loco, email krivdaj@gmail.com. Please include the app version, device model and a short description. You do not need to send passwords or your Apple Account details.

Using the app offline

Conjugations, examples, practice and pronunciation work without an internet connection. iCloud synchronization needs an internet connection and the same Apple Account on your devices.

iCloud synchronization

Check that iCloud is available on both devices and that synchronization is enabled in Verbo Loco settings. Leave the app open briefly after reconnecting. You can disable synchronization and continue learning locally.

Daily reminder and audio

Daily reminders are optional local notifications. Manage permission in system settings. For pronunciation, check your media volume and audio output.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 18 or later. See our privacy policy for details about local storage and iCloud.
