Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2, 2025

Overview

Verbo Loco ("the App") is designed with your privacy in mind. We believe in transparency and want you to understand exactly how your data is handled.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. The App operates entirely on your device without sending any information to external servers.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device:

  • Favorites: Your saved verbs are stored on your device
  • Practice Progress: Quiz history and scores remain local
  • Settings: Your preferences are stored on your device

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your data (favorites, progress, settings) is synced through your personal iCloud account. This data:

  • Is stored in your private iCloud container
  • Is protected by Apple's security measures
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Can be disabled at any time in Settings

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
